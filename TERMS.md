# Screenshot to PDF Terms of Use

Screenshot to PDF, maintained by [davidlewismakes](https://github.com/davidlewismakes), combines selected PNG, JPEG, and WebP files into a PDF. Use images you have permission to process. Review the generated PDF for quality and page order before sharing or relying on it, and keep your original images.

The converter is provided as available. The ChatGPT integration also uses OpenAI's service, which is subject to [OpenAI's terms](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/).

For help or to report a problem, use [project support](https://github.com/davidlewismakes/screenshot-to-pdf/issues). Please keep personal information and private files out of public issues.

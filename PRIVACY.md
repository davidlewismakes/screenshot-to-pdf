# Screenshot to PDF Privacy Policy

Screenshot to PDF is maintained by [davidlewismakes](https://github.com/davidlewismakes).

Images you choose are processed in your browser. The Screenshot to PDF server does not receive or store those images or the generated PDF. Files remain in the converter until you clear them or close it. A downloaded PDF is saved by your browser.

Vercel hosts the website and MCP endpoint. Requests to those services may be processed by Vercel for delivery, operation, and security. The plugin has no separate account system, advertising, or analytics.

If you use the plugin through ChatGPT, OpenAI processes your use of ChatGPT. If you click **Save to ChatGPT Library**, ChatGPT receives the generated PDF. If you attach the PDF to a chat yourself, ChatGPT also receives it. Review [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and your ChatGPT data controls for those actions.

You can remove files from the converter before creating a PDF, delete downloaded copies locally, and manage uploaded files in ChatGPT. For questions, see [project support](https://github.com/davidlewismakes/screenshot-to-pdf/issues). Please keep private screenshots and personal details out of public issues.
